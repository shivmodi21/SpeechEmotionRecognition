UNDER THE HOOD

How the model works

01

Audio

The uploaded recording is prepared as the input for two complementary model branches.

02

Acoustic branch

A 550-dimensional handcrafted acoustic representation captures complementary characteristics of speech.

03

CNN branch

A normalized 128 × 256 × 1 log-Mel spectrogram is processed by a convolutional neural network.

04

Fusion

Both learned representations are combined before the final four-class emotion prediction.

550 ACOUSTIC FEATURES

What the model listens for

The handcrafted branch combines complementary acoustic descriptors. Time-varying features are summarized using their mean and standard deviation, while MFCCs also include first- and second-order temporal deltas.

240 MFCC + delta + delta-delta
24 Chroma statistics
256 Mel-spectrogram statistics
14 Spectral contrast statistics
12 Tonnetz statistics
4 Polynomial feature statistics

128 × 256 × 1 CNN FEATURES

Visualizing the voice

The CNN branch represents the audio as a normalized log-Mel spectrogram. This time-frequency representation allows the convolutional network to learn local patterns in the speech signal.
